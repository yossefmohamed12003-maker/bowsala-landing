# CLOCAL Burgundy Hoodie — Nano Banana Pro Prompt Pack (Gemini app)

## How to run it

1. Open **gemini.google.com** → new chat → in the model picker choose **Thinking** / **Pro** (this is what runs Nano Banana Pro for images; the "Fast" model uses the weaker Nano Banana).
2. Attach **the hoodie photo** (the mannequin one).
3. Paste **Prompt 0 (Setup)** + **Shot 01** together as the first message.
4. When Shot 01 looks right, **stay in the same chat** for every other shot. Gemini keeps the same face and room much better inside one conversation.
5. Ask for **one image per message**. If an image looks AI, use the fix lines at the bottom instead of starting over.
6. Download each image with the download button (not a screenshot) to keep full resolution.

> If the face starts drifting after ~8 images, start a new chat, attach the hoodie photo **and** your best approved shot, and write: "Same man as image 2, same hoodie as image 1." Then continue with the next shot.

## Prompt 0 — Setup (paste once, together with Shot 01)

```
You are photographing a real fashion shoot for the independent Cairo streetwear label CLOCAL. Every image must look like an unretouched photo from a real full-frame camera, not an AI image.

THE GARMENT (the attached photo is the exact product — do not redesign it):
A plain burgundy / deep wine-red heavyweight cotton fleece pullover hoodie. Oversized cut, dropped shoulders, double-layer hood with NO drawstrings, large kangaroo pocket, wide ribbed cuffs and a ribbed hem band in the same color. No logo, no print, no text, no embroidery. The small white circle with "L" on the mannequin is a size sticker — do not include it.
Fit: size L, body length 76 cm, chest width 64 cm laid flat. On the model it is roomy: hem sits just below the hips, shoulder seams drop past the shoulder, sleeves stack a little above the cuffs. Matte brushed fleece with real folds, creases, slight pilling, and tension where the body pushes against it.

THE MODEL (keep him identical in every shot):
Egyptian / Mediterranean young man, early 20s, lean with slightly broad shoulders, long limbs. Strong jaw, slightly asymmetric face with character, tired relaxed eyes, light uneven stubble, a few small skin blemishes, visible pores. Dark messy unstyled hair. Black straight-leg trousers, worn black suede sneakers. Not a runway model — a real kid from the underground streetwear scene. Subtle, almost candid expressions; often caught between poses.

THE LOCATION (same in every shot):
An old, used Cairo apartment that a creative person lives in. Warm faded off-white walls with uneven plaster and small marks (aged, not ruined). A dark brown vintage leather sofa with creased, worn leather. A faded burgundy Persian rug, slightly crooked, on old patterned floor tiles. A plain chrome side table with a few art books stacked unevenly. An old floor lamp. A potted monstera near a tall window with a view of Cairo buildings.

LIGHT AND CAMERA:
One light source only: soft daylight from that window. Parts of the room fall into natural shadow. Neutral true-to-life colors and skin tones, normal contrast, light film grain, slight lens softness at the edges, moderate depth of field with the background still readable. Framing can be slightly imperfect.

AVOID: waxy or perfect skin, plastic fabric, over-sharpening, glowing edges, HDR, orange-teal or purple grading, heavy bokeh, perfect symmetry, dramatic studio lighting, posed catalog look.

Confirm you understand, then make Shot 01.
```

## Fix lines (use when an image looks AI)

- **Skin too smooth:** `Keep everything the same but make the skin real: visible pores, uneven texture, small blemishes, no smoothing.`
- **Fabric looks plastic:** `Keep everything the same but make the hoodie matte brushed cotton fleece with soft irregular folds and slight pilling, not shiny or smooth.`
- **Wrong hoodie** (logo, drawstrings, wrong color): `The hoodie must match the attached product photo exactly: plain burgundy, no drawstrings, no logo, kangaroo pocket, ribbed cuffs and hem. Fix only the hoodie.`
- **Hands look wrong:** `Keep everything the same and fix his hands: natural relaxed fingers, five per hand, real knuckles and nails.`
- **Too cinematic / too perfect:** `Make this look like an unedited photo: flatter natural light, neutral colors, less contrast, light grain, no glow, slightly imperfect framing.`
- **Face drifted:** `His face changed. Make him look exactly like the man in Shot 01 — same face, same hair, same stubble.`
- **Hoodie fits too tight / too short:** `The hoodie is size L oversized: 76 cm long, 64 cm wide flat. Make it roomier, hem just below the hips, shoulders dropped.`
